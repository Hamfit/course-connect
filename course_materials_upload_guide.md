## ⚡ How to Upload Materials (Admin Guide)

Since you are authenticated with the **Admin** role, CourseConnect bypasses the standard moderation queue for your uploads:

1. **Log In** to your admin account on CourseConnect.
2. Go to the [Upload Page](http://localhost:5173/upload).
3. Select **University of Lagos (UNILAG)**.
4. Select **Computer Science** as Department.
5. Select the **Level** (e.g. 500 Level) and **Semester** (First or Second Semester).
6. Pick the **Course** from the dropdown list.
7. Upload your file (PDF, notes, slides, MP4, etc.).
8. Enter a suitable title and a clear description. 
   * *Tip: If it's a Past Question, prefix the title/description with "[PQ]" (e.g., "[PQ] CSC 313 Exam Past Question 2018/2019 Session") to distinguish it.*
9. Click **Upload Materials**.
10. The material is **instantly live** on the portal, bypassing the moderation check.
